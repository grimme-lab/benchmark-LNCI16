## Data Sources and References

This benchmark is a curated collection of molecular structures from original work and previously published datasets.

### How to cite

When using this benchmark:

1. Always cite our publication:
   
   "Efficient Computation of the Interaction Energies of Very Large Non-covalently Bound Complexes." <br>
   J. Gorges, B. Bädorf, S. Grimme, A. Hansen, Synlett 2023, 34, 1135–1146. DOI: 10.1055/s-0042-1753141

3. Additionally, cite the original sources listed below for the corresponding structures.
